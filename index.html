KNOWLEDGE HUB

Blood DonationFacts

Things everyone should know about giving blood.

🩸

Every 2 Seconds

Someone in India needs blood. That’s over 43,000 units required every single day nationwide.

💉

1 Donation = 3 Lives

Your single donation is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets — each helping a different patient.

⏱️

Just 45 Minutes

The entire donation process takes under an hour. Your body replaces the fluid within 24 hours.

🔁

Donate Every 56 Days

Healthy adults can safely donate whole blood every 56 days — that’s up to 6 times a year.

🏥

No Substitute Exists

Blood cannot be manufactured artificially. Human donation remains the only source of life-saving blood products.

📉

30% Deficit in India

India has a chronic shortfall of nearly 3 million blood units annually, with rural areas most severely affected.

❤️

Heart Health Bonus

Regular donors have a lower risk of heart attacks. Donating blood reduces iron levels and oxidative stress.

🧪

Free Health Checkup

Every donation includes screening for HIV, hepatitis B & C, syphilis, and malaria — completely free.
