How long does blood donation take?

The complete process usually takes around 30–45 minutes, while the actual blood collection takes only 8–10 minutes.
2

Who can donate blood?

Healthy adults aged 18–65 with sufficient hemoglobin and body weight can generally donate blood.
3

Is blood donation safe?

Yes. Sterile disposable needles are used, making the process completely safe.
4

How often can I donate blood?

Whole blood donation is usually allowed every 56 days for healthy adults.
5

Will I get weak after donating?

Most people feel normal after resting briefly and drinking enough fluids.
