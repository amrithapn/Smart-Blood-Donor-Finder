About BloodBridge

BloodBridge was created with a simple mission: to connect blood donors with patients instantly through technology, reducing delays during emergencies and saving more lives across India.

Our Mission

To ensure no life is lost due to blood shortage by building India’s most intelligent real-time donor network.

Our Vision

A future where every patient receives the right blood within minutes, regardless of location or background.
